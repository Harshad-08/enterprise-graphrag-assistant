# 1. Purpose
This policy defines how long the company keeps business records and when they are deleted.

# 2. Retention Periods
Customer data must be retained for 7 years after the end of the customer contract.
Application logs must be retained for 12 months.
Database backups must be retained for no longer than 35 days.
Financial records must be retained for 10 years.

# 3. Deletion
Customer deletion requests must be completed within 30 days of verification.
Data that has reached the end of its retention period must be securely deleted.
