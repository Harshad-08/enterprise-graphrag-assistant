# 1. Purpose
These guidelines describe baseline network protections for corporate and remote connections.

# 2. Remote Connections
VPN sessions must be disconnected after 15 minutes of inactivity.
Split tunnelling is not permitted on corporate VPN connections.

# 3. Network Segmentation
Production systems must be isolated from the office network.
