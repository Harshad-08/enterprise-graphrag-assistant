# 1. Scope
This standard governs remote connections to the corporate network through the Corporate VPN.

# 2. VPN Sessions
VPN sessions are disconnected after 60 minutes of inactivity.
The Corporate VPN client must be updated within 14 days of a new release.

# 3. Personal Devices
Personal devices are permitted for remote access only when accessing the webmail portal through a browser.
