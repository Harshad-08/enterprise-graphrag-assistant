# 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the rules for employees who work from home.

# 2. Eligibility
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

# 3. Devices and Remote Access
Employees may use personal devices for remote access to internal systems.
Remote connections must use the Corporate VPN.
