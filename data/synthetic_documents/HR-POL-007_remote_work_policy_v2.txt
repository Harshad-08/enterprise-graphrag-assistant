# 1. Purpose
This policy defines how employees may work from home and connect to company resources. It replaces version 1.0 of the Remote Work Policy, which is no longer in force.

# 2. Eligibility and Schedule
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Employees must be available during core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 local time on remote days.
Working from outside the home country requires prior approval from Human Resources and is limited to 30 days per year.

# 3. Devices and Remote Access
Employees must use a company-managed laptop for all remote work.
Personal devices are prohibited for remote access to internal systems.
All remote connections must use the Corporate VPN with multi-factor authentication.
