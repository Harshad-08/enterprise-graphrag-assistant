# 1. Purpose
This procedure describes how a new external partner is connected to the Public API Gateway. This procedure depends on the API Authentication Standard.

# 2. Steps
1. Business Development records the partner agreement in the partner register.
2. Platform Engineering creates an OAuth 2.0 client for the partner in the Public API Gateway.
3. The partner receives its client secret through the secure credential portal.
4. Platform Engineering issues a mutual TLS certificate to the partner.
5. The partner completes a test integration in the sandbox environment within 10 business days.

# 3. Ownership
Business Development owns the Partner Integration Onboarding Procedure.
