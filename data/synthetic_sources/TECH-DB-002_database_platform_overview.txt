1. Atlas Overview
Atlas is a production database cluster that stores customer orders for the Order Management Service. The Atlas Cluster runs PostgreSQL 15 across three availability zones. Platform Engineering owns the Atlas Cluster.

2. Architecture
Atlas uses one primary node and two synchronous replicas. Failover to a replica completes in under 60 seconds. Connection pooling for Atlas is handled by PgBouncer.
=== PAGE BREAK ===
3. Backups and Recovery
Atlas backups are taken nightly and kept for 35 days. Point-in-time recovery is available for the previous 7 days.

4. Monitoring
Atlas metrics are exported to the central monitoring stack. Site Reliability Engineering is paged when replication lag exceeds 30 seconds.
